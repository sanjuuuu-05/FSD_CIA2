    About this tool

    OSM Route Explorer finds a place with Nominatim, calculates a car, bike or walking route with public OSRM servers and draws it with Leaflet.

    The cyan outline is a simplified India boundary used to limit where you can search and click. It is approximate and not an authoritative map of international borders.

    Estimates ignore live traffic. Long inter-state routes can take a while on the free demo server.
