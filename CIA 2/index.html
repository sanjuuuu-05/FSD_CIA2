    About this tool

    OSM Route Explorer finds a place with Nominatim, calculates a car, bike or walking route with public OSRM servers and draws it with Leaflet.

    This prototype covers the Bengaluru–Coimbatore region only. The dashed cyan box shows where you can search and click.

    Estimates ignore live traffic. The free routing servers can be slow at busy times.
